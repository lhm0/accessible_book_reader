# Contributing

Thank you for considering a contribution to the Accessible Book Reader.

## Licence model

This repository uses several public licences. See `LICENSE.md` for the exact
scope:

- software: `GPL-3.0-or-later`
- hardware designs: `CERN-OHL-S-2.0`
- documentation and project-owned media: `CC-BY-SA-4.0`
- third-party components: their existing licences

Do not submit material that you are not entitled to contribute under these
terms. Clearly identify third-party material and retain all required notices.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before a contribution can be merged, every contributor must agree to the
Contributor License Agreement in `CLA.md`. The CLA does not transfer
copyright. It gives the maintainer sufficient rights to:

- publish the contribution under the applicable public project licence;
- keep the project's strong-copyleft model consistent; and
- offer the project, including the contribution, under separate commercial
  terms for future development partnerships.

For a contribution made as part of employment, the contributor must have the
employer's permission or the employer must sign an appropriate entity CLA.

## Contribution requirements

- Base changes on the current `main` branch.
- Add or update tests where practical.
- Update relevant documentation.
- State the origin and licence of every added media or third-party file.
- Do not include credentials, personal data, copyrighted book scans, vendor
  datasheets or generated local caches.
- Review [PATENT_NOTICE.md](PATENT_NOTICE.md). Do not knowingly submit material
  covered by third-party rights that you are not entitled to license, and
  disclose any patent or other right known to be relevant to the contribution.
- Describe significant modifications to LGPL-covered PN5180 files as required
  by that licence.

Opening an issue or discussing an idea does not by itself grant the project a
licence to use that idea or any attached copyrighted material.
