## Responsibility of users and manufacturers

Anyone who makes, uses, modifies, imports, offers or distributes the project
must assess the legal requirements in every relevant jurisdiction. A
commercial manufacturer or distributor should obtain a professional
freedom-to-operate analysis before investing in production or market entry.
That analysis should cover the concrete implementation, components, target
countries and intended activities; the publication of this repository is not
a substitute for it.

German patent law currently excludes acts done privately and for
non-commercial purposes from the effects of a patent under
[section 11(1) of the German Patent Act](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/patg/__11.html).
Both conditions and the applicable jurisdiction matter. Commercial activity
is not covered by that exception. The exclusive rights associated with a
German patent are described in
[section 9 of the German Patent Act](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/patg/__9.html).
Users outside Germany must not assume that an equivalent exception exists.

The German Patent and Trade Mark Office provides
[DEPATISnet](https://www.dpma.de/english/search/depatisnet/index.html) for
searching patent publications. A self-conducted database search may identify
obvious documents but does not provide the assurance of a professional
freedom-to-operate opinion.
